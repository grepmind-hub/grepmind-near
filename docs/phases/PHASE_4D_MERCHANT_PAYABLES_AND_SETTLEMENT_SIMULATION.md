## Accounting and compatibility decisions

- All monetary values are integer minor units with explicit currency. Commission policies are versioned; no default 10% rate was adopted from the illustrative prompt.
- Platform selling price, partner supply price, commission, payable, rider cash liability, rider earnings, provider capture/settlement and external merchant transfer are separate concepts.
- Accrual is limited to eligible delivered INR platform orders whose payment is verified sandbox UPI capture or whose synthetic COD collection and rider remittance are verified. Supply basis is read from the immutable order snapshot.
- Adjustments are append-only and signed by accounting direction. A payable snapshot includes adjustments; reconciliation does not count them twice. Approval and settlement stages require distinct authorized actors where configured.
- A QA funding pool or verified synthetic COD remittance is only an accounting simulation source. It does not authorize or represent a bank transfer.
- Legacy `customer_orders.payment_status` remains a compatibility projection; this phase does not backfill historical payments or reinterpret `not_collected` records. Phase 3F rider earnings remain independent.

## Implemented API surface

Admin routes are registered under `/api/v1/admin/finance/` for policies, eligible orders, accrual, payables, adjustments, QA funding pools, settlements, transitions and reconciliation. Merchant read routes are `/api/v1/merchant/businesses/{businessId}/finance/payables` and `/api/v1/merchant/businesses/{businessId}/finance/settlements`. The exact request/response schemas are maintained in `api/openapi.yaml` and route handlers in `backend/cmd/api/main.go`.
